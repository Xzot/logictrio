Every day the poster prints three new puzzles, the same for everyone. Each has exactly one solution, and you never have to guess. When you're stuck, the first tap on Hint hatches the cells to look at; the second explains the reasoning and makes the move.

Crowns

  1. Put one crown in every row, every column and every colored region.
  2. Crowns never touch, not even corner to corner.
  3. Tap once for a ✕, twice for a crown. Swipe to drop several ✕ at once.

Binary

  1. Fill every cell with a sun or a moon.
  2. Never more than two of the same side by side, across or down.
  3. Every row and column holds as many suns as moons. Cells joined by = match; × means they differ.

Path

  1. Draw one line from 1 that passes through every cell exactly once.
  2. Meet the numbers in order and finish on the last one.
  3. Drag back over the line to take steps back, or tap a cell of the line to cut it there.

Questions

What is the day run?

The number of days in a row you've solved at least one of that day's puzzles. A missed day is covered by a freeze if you have one left that week: one a week for everyone, two with Premium.

Does my progress move to a new iPhone or my iPad?

Yes, from version 1.1. Saved puzzles, solve times and your day run sync through your own iCloud account when iCloud is on for Logic Trio (iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud). Nothing goes through our servers; we don't have any.

How do I restore Premium?

Turn the poster over (the arrows at the top right), then tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel the subscription?

iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Logic Trio. Premium stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget and pick Logic Trio. The Home Screen widget shows today's three stripes; the Lock Screen one shows your day run.

Something's wrong with a puzzle

Write to shchukavlad4@gmail.com with the date and the game. Every puzzle is checked by a solver before it ships, so a report helps us find the rare one that slipped through.
