How do the daily puzzles work, and when do they change?

Every player gets the same three puzzles on the same day. A new set appears at midnight in your local time zone. Past dailies stay in the Archive, where you can open them from the calendar. Free players can see the last 7 days; Premium opens the full archive.

How do hints work?

Tap the hint button once and the board highlights the area where the next logical step is. Tap it again and the app explains that step in plain words, for example “This row has only one cell left for a crown,” and makes the move for you. Free players get 3 hints a day. Premium hints are unlimited.

How do streaks and Streak Freezes work?

Your streak grows by one for every day you solve at least one of that day's dailies. Archive puzzles don't count toward it. If you miss a day, a Streak Freeze is used automatically and your streak survives. Everyone gets 1 freeze a week for free; Premium gets 2. Unused freezes don't carry over, and the allowance resets every Monday.

How do I add the widgets?

Lock Screen (streak and today's progress):

  1. Touch and hold your Lock Screen until Customize appears.
  2. Tap Customize, then Lock Screen.
  3. Tap the widget area under the clock, find Logic Trio and pick a widget.
  4. Tap Done.

Home Screen (streak plus the three games, tap one to play):

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit in the top corner, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for Logic Trio, choose a size and tap Add Widget.

What's free, and what does Premium add?

Free: all of today's dailies, 3 Practice puzzles per game per day, 3 hints a day and the last 7 days of the archive. There are no ads.

Premium adds unlimited Practice in all four difficulties, the full archive, unlimited hints, the Ink Blue, Sage and Plum themes, alternate app icons, detailed stats and 2 Streak Freezes a week. It's $2.99 a month or $19.99 a year (with a 7-day free trial), or $39.99 once for Lifetime. Prices may vary by country.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases (it's also on the Premium screen). Make sure you're signed in with the Apple ID you used to buy. Premium isn't shared through Family Sharing.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Logic Trio Premium and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for. For refunds, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I need Game Center?

No. Game Center is optional. If you're signed in, you can compare times on the daily leaderboards for each game and collect 18 achievements. Everything else works without it, and the whole app works offline.
