Privacy

Effective October 29, 2026 (version 1.1)

Logic Trio (“the app”) is made by Vladyslav Shchuka (“we”). In short: we collect nothing. Below is where each kind of data is kept.

No data comes to us

We run no servers, have no accounts, and use no ads, analytics or tracking. We never see your puzzles, times or purchases.

On your iPhone

Puzzles in progress, solve times, your day run and your settings are stored on the device. The day run and today's results are also kept in a container shared with the app's widgets, so the widgets can show them.

In your iCloud (version 1.1 and later)

If iCloud is on for Logic Trio, your saved puzzles go to your private iCloud database and your day run to iCloud key-value storage, so they appear on your other devices. This storage belongs to your Apple ID and is operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we can't read it. Turn it off in iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud.

Purchases

Premium is sold by Apple through the App Store. We never see payment details; the app only asks the App Store which Logic Trio purchases your Apple ID holds.

Game Center

Optional. If you're signed in, the app posts your times for today's puzzles to Game Center leaderboards and reports achievements. Apple runs Game Center; we receive nothing.

The morning bell

The daily reminder is a local notification set on your iPhone, only after you allow it. Turn it off on the back of the poster or in iPhone Settings.

Children

The app is rated 4+. It collects no information from anyone, children included.
