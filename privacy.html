Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Logic Trio: Daily Puzzles (“the app”) is developed by Vladyslav Shchuka (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

We don't collect your data

The app does not collect any personal information, and we have no servers that receive it. We don't sell or share data with anyone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your puzzle progress, solve times, stats, streak and settings are stored only on your iPhone. The streak and today's progress are kept in storage shared between the app and its widgets (an App Group) so the widgets can show them. This data never leaves your device through us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We never see your payment details. The app only asks Apple which purchases your Apple ID owns, so it can unlock Premium.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you're signed in to Game Center, the app sends your daily puzzle times to its leaderboards and reports achievements. Game Center is operated by Apple, and your Game Center name and scores are handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive any of it.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone. The app asks for permission first, and you can turn it off anytime in the app or in iPhone Settings. No push service is involved.

Network

All puzzles work offline. The app goes online only for StoreKit (loading prices, buying and restoring purchases) and Game Center (if you use it).

Children

The app is rated 4+ and suitable for all ages. It doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
